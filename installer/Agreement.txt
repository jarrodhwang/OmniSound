OmniSound Lite mirrors a Windows output without installing an audio driver.
OmniSound Pro installs a virtual audio driver and a device management service.
Pro installation requires administrator access and an appropriately signed
driver package. Setup does not disable Windows security features.

Audio is processed locally. The microphone is used only during an explicitly
started calibration, and calibration does not save recording files. Settings,
device identifiers, diagnostic counters and setup logs may be stored locally.
Uninstall retains audio settings so they can be reused after reinstallation.

Playback latency and synchronization depend on Windows, device buffers,
transport and the listening position. Zero latency and universally exact
synchronization are not promised. Stereo positioning is a PCM speaker mapping
feature; licensed third-party spatial audio formats remain separate products.

Development builds have not completed all device, certification or release
validation. Use the supplied status and measurement information to determine
whether a particular device arrangement meets your requirements.
